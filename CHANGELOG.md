# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-23)

First release. Simulator Upgrade brings together:

- **Equipment in the simulator**: F3, or Start on a pad, opens the game's main menu on the course list, the stage
  picker, the team screen and "Begin challenge?", with an Equipment entry in the simulator's key guide.
- **Custom Training**, a course on the Combat Training tab:
  - any of 206 opponents, one to three copies, a mixed group of up to three different opponents, or a pre-built round
    from any simulator course;
  - Passive (held, everything refills), Aggressive (real conditions: no refills, Auto-Life only, enemies stay dead
    until Respawn) or Healing (held, the party at 1% HP);
  - Easy, Normal, Dynamic or Hard;
  - everything changeable mid-course from a settings menu in the game's own choice window (F7, or LB + RB on a pad),
    with Respawn (F4, or the menu's first row);
  - Zack and Sephiroth can join the team.
  It replaces Training Dummy's three courses.
- **Meters** in Custom Training: damage, damage taken and healing per second, by character, with the wave number and
  the last clear time in Aggressive. Battle Meters' and Training Dummy's features in one panel.
- **Simulator Stages 1.3.1**, included: stages load without other mods (the fix for the Steam reports, which affected
  Epic too). Custom Training can use every stage; opponents and rounds that need more room
  than a stage has are marked in the menu.
- A compatibility pak for Junon Colosseum players.
